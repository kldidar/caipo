# Indicators

## Three layers
The Indicators page compares countries in three separate layers: layer A, structural change; layer B, digital transformation; and layer C, AI-specific outcomes. Layers are reported on their own for each country. They are never combined into an index or an overall verdict, and a layer is never filled from another.

## Coverage and missing values
A coverage table shows, for each layer and country, what is recorded. "No indicator recorded" means that no indicator for that layer has been recorded here from the sources searched; it is not evidence that no reliable measure exists. A year without a value is left empty and is never filled in.

## An indicator's page
An indicator's page shows its definition as published, its layer, its provenance (source, original provider, terms, retrieval date, file, and SHA-256), and its values by country. Values are shown rounded to two decimals and are stored exactly as published. Above the values, a separate panel shows the formal events of the core AI policies of the same countries on the same years; it does not show that any policy caused any change.

## Where the values come from
The reference values are World Bank World Development Indicators for Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan, used under CC BY 4.0. The World Bank does not endorse this project.

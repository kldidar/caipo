# Accounts and roles

## Roles
Every account has one or more of four roles. A Reader browses the workspace, reads the registry, and may write articles. A Researcher can also register sources and documents, add policies and indicators, run analyses, and record searches. A Reviewer can also publish or decline contributed articles. An Administrator manages accounts and roles.

## Two-step verification
Reviewer and Administrator accounts sign in with a password and a code from an authenticator app. Any account can turn on two-step verification on the Two-step verification page.

## Articles
Signed-in users can write an article and send it for review. A Reviewer or Administrator publishes it or declines it with a reason. A published article is labelled as its author's view, is published under CC BY 4.0, and is never treated as evidence.

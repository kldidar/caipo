# Searches

## Why searches are recorded
The Searches page records searches for sources: what was searched for, where, how, and what was found. This is how CAIPO records that a source was not found: as the outcome of a search bounded by what was examined, never as a document.

## What a search record holds
A search record names the layer searched for, the countries, the date, the queries, the sources in scope, the inclusion and exclusion criteria, the sources examined, the method, and the outcome with a summary.

## Outcomes
A search ends in one of three outcomes: no qualifying source found, qualifying sources found, or inconclusive. "No qualifying source found" is limited to the sources examined; it does not show that no such source exists anywhere.

# About CAIPO

## What CAIPO is
CAIPO, the Central Asia AI Policy Observatory, is a research platform for the study "AI Policy and Economic Transformation in Central Asia: A Comparative Analysis of Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan". It records public policy sources with their provenance, keeps the core AI policy corpus apart from broader contextual policy, compares countries on economic indicators in three separate layers, and runs reproducible, descriptive analyses.

## Reference data and demonstration data
Every record is either reference data or demonstration data, and the two are never mixed. Reference data come from real public sources: the metadata of legal documents and World Bank indicator values for Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan. Demonstration data are synthetic records of two synthetic countries, DEMO Northland and DEMO Southland, which exist to show what the software does and say nothing about any real country. Pages and exports label each record with its class.

## What CAIPO does not claim
CAIPO makes no causal claim. It never states or implies that a policy caused an economic outcome. Comparisons and analyses are descriptive, and every comparison carries a notice that it is not a causal analysis. An empty list or a missing value says only that nothing has been recorded here; it is not evidence that something does not exist.

## Where to start
The Overview page shows what the workspace holds. From there, the Policies page lists the core AI policy corpus and the contextual policy registry, the Indicators page compares the countries layer by layer, the Analysis page holds reproducible analyses, and the Searches page records searches for sources.

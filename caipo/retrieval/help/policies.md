# Policies

## Core corpus and contextual registry
The Policies page has two tabs. The core AI policy corpus holds policies whose subject is artificial intelligence. The contextual policy registry holds broader policies, such as digital economy or telecommunications programmes, which are never counted as AI policy. Every assignment to one or the other is provisional and is shown with its reason, until the research protocol's eligibility rules are settled.

## Filters
The list can be filtered by country, by data class, and by kind of policy. Apply sets the filters and Clear removes them. A table below the list shows what is recorded for each country.

## A policy's status
A policy's formal status is derived from its formal events: adopted, amended, entered into force, expired, and repealed. It is never stored or typed in. Implementation is not a formal event. Each event names the document that establishes it and, where known, the article or paragraph.

## Provisions
For a policy of the core corpus, the policy page lists its AI-related provisions: objectives, instruments, and institutional arrangements, each with the document and the locator it comes from. Only demonstration documents show quoted text; the text of a real legal document is not held.

## Export
"Export the register (CSV)" on the Policies page downloads the policy register as a CSV file.

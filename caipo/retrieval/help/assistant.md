# The assistants

## Two assistants
CAIPO has two assistants. The research assistant answers questions about the World Bank values held for Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan and about the synthetic texts of the demonstration documents. The help assistant, opened from the button at the bottom right of a page, answers questions about CAIPO from these help pages.

## Answers come only from evidence
Each statement in an answer cites the evidence it comes from: a recorded value, a passage of a demonstration document, or a section of these help pages. The links are made by the server, never written by the model, so an answer cannot show a reference that does not exist. Quotations and numbers are checked against the stored evidence, and a statement that fails a check is removed before the answer is shown.

## When there is not enough evidence
When the evidence does not answer a question, the assistant says so instead of answering. It does not answer from general knowledge, and it never states that a policy caused an outcome.

## Limits
Answers are given in English or Russian. There is a daily limit on questions. Questions are sent to the assistants' language-model provider, Groq, to be answered, so a question should not contain personal information.
